MyLoom Recorder

Connect to your MyLoom

This extension records into your own installation. It never sends anything anywhere else.

How it works

  • Click the toolbar icon on any page, choose a mode, and record.
  • A control bar appears on the page with a timer, pause and stop.
  • Chunks upload while you record, so long sessions are safe.
  • When you stop, the share page opens with your link ready.
  • Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+L starts and stops.
